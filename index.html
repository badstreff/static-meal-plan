BreakfastApple & brown sugar steel-cut oatsCook to package · 1 breakfast+

Warm oats, a chopped apple, and a little richness. Your protein shake stays on the side.

~630 kcal per meal~48 g protein

What you need

  • 60 g dry steel-cut oats + water per package
  • 1 medium apple, chopped
  • 2 tsp brown sugar
  • 10 g butter OR ¼ cup half-and-half
  • Protein powder providing 40 g protein + water
  • Cinnamon, optional

How to make it

  1. Simmer the oats in water for the time on your package, stirring occasionally.
  2. Stir in the apple near the end for softer fruit, or add it fresh. Finish with brown sugar and butter or half-and-half.
  3. Shake the powder with water separately. Use its label to measure 40 g of protein, not 40 g of powder.

Make fresh on days 1 and 3. Measure oats dry; use your powder and dairy labels for tracking.

BreakfastHam & spinach frittata with toastAbout 25 min · 1 breakfast+

Eggs, diced ham, cottage cheese and Parmesan, with toast on the side.

~650 kcal per meal~50 g protein

What you need

  • 4 large eggs
  • 2 oz / 57 g diced cooked ham
  • ¼ cup low-fat cottage cheese
  • ¼ cup grated Parmesan or Pecorino
  • ½ cup chopped baby spinach
  • 2 slices whole-grain bread (~160 calories total)
  • Cooking spray and black pepper

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F / 175°C. Coat a small oven-safe skillet with cooking spray; warm the ham.
  2. Whisk eggs, cottage cheese, Parmesan and spinach. Pour into the skillet, cook briefly to set the base, then bake until fully set (about 16–18 minutes; 160°F / 71°C in the center).
  3. Toast the bread and serve with the whole frittata. Ham and Parmesan add salt; season to taste.

Cook fresh on days 2 and 4. Ham replaces the Italian chicken sausage; its label determines the final calories.

LunchBuffalo chicken rice bowlsAbout 30 min · 2 lunches+

Chicken, black beans and brown rice with buffalo sauce and melted cheddar.

~727 kcal per meal~66 g protein

What you need

  • 1 lb / 454 g raw chicken breast, diced
  • 2 cups cooked brown rice
  • ⅔ cup drained black beans
  • ⅓ cup diced red onion
  • ⅓ cup buffalo sauce
  • ⅓ cup shredded part-skim cheddar
  • ⅔ tsp olive oil for cooking + 4 tsp for portions
  • ⅓ tsp each paprika and cumin; scallions, salt and pepper

How to make it

  1. Cook the onion in ⅔ tsp oil until softened. Stir in beans, paprika and cumin.
  2. In a sprayed skillet, cook chicken to 165°F / 74°C. Toss with buffalo sauce.
  3. Split rice, beans, chicken and cheddar between two containers. Mix 2 tsp extra olive oil into each rice/bean portion; finish with scallions.

Cook on day 1; eat on days 1 and 3. The extra olive oil is included in the calorie estimate.

LunchGreek chicken rice bowlsAbout 1 hr incl. marinade · 2 lunches+

Lemon-oregano chicken with rice, crunchy vegetables, olives and feta.

~697 kcal per meal~73 g protein

What you need

  • 1¼ lb / 567 g raw chicken breast
  • 1 cup + 2 tsp dry quick-cooking brown rice
  • 1⅔ cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1⅔ tbsp each lemon juice and red wine vinegar
  • 2½ tsp olive oil for marinade + 3⅓ tsp for serving
  • 2–3 garlic cloves; 1⅔ tsp dried oregano
  • About ⅚ pint cherry tomatoes; ⅚ cucumber; ⅚ bell pepper
  • 17 Kalamata olives; 3 tbsp + 1 tsp feta; lemon wedges

How to make it

  1. Flatten chicken to an even thickness. Marinate in lemon juice, vinegar, marinade oil, garlic and oregano for 30 minutes in the fridge.
  2. Cook rice with broth according to its package. Grill or pan-cook chicken to 165°F / 74°C; rest and slice.
  3. Split chicken and rice between two portions. Pack chopped vegetables, olives and feta separately. At serving, add the toppings, remaining oil and lemon.

Cook on day 2; eat on days 2 and 4. Reheat only chicken and rice; add the cold toppings afterward.

DinnerHoney garlic chicken thighsAbout 1 hr incl. marinade · 2 dinners+

Glazed chicken thighs, rice and easy frozen mixed vegetables.

~770 kcal per meal~72 g protein

What you need

  • 1⅔ lb / 757 g raw boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • 2½ cups cooked brown rice
  • ⅔ lb / 303 g frozen mixed vegetables
  • ⅔ medium sweet onion, sliced
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce; 1⅔ tbsp honey
  • 2⅔ tsp soy sauce
  • ⅔ tsp each sesame oil and sriracha
  • 1–2 garlic cloves, minced

How to make it

  1. Mix sauces, honey, sesame oil and garlic. Coat chicken and refrigerate for 30 minutes.
  2. Roast at 425°F / 220°C on a lined tray; add onion and finish under the broiler for color. Check chicken reaches 165°F / 74°C; watch the broiler closely.
  3. Heat vegetables and rice. Divide all food equally into two dinner containers.

Cook on day 1; eat on days 1 and 4. Mixed vegetables replace broccoli, so actual nutrition varies slightly.

DinnerChile lemon chicken rice bowlsAbout 1 hr · 2 dinners+

Yogurt-seasoned thighs with peppers, cabbage, tomato and rice.

~789 kcal per meal~73 g protein

What you need

  • 1⅓ lb / 605 g raw boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • 2 cups cooked brown rice; 100 g nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 2 tsp each chili powder, onion powder, garlic powder for chicken
  • 2 tsp lemon juice for chicken + 2 tsp for vegetables
  • About 1⅓ bell peppers; ⅔ small yellow onion
  • 8 oz shredded cabbage; 5⅓ oz tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp + 1 tsp olive oil; 2 tsp minced garlic
  • ⅔ tsp each chili and onion powder for vegetables; salt
  • Optional cilantro and fresh chile

How to make it

  1. Coat thighs with yogurt, chicken spices and lemon juice. Roast at 425°F / 220°C; broil briefly for browning. Check 165°F / 74°C before slicing.
  2. Heat oil, garlic and vegetable spices in a skillet. Add diced peppers and onion; soften, then add cabbage and tomato sauce.
  3. Stir in rice, sliced chicken and remaining lemon juice. Divide evenly into two containers.

Cook on day 2; eat on days 2 and 3. Bagged shredded cabbage keeps preparation simple.

SnackWhipped cottage cheese & berries10 min · 2 full-size snacks+

Smooth cottage cheese topped with berries and slivered almonds.

~330 kcal per meal~33 g protein

What you need

  • 16 oz / 454 g low-fat cottage cheese
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract; zero-calorie sweetener to taste
  • 1 cup strawberries + 1 cup other berries
  • 3 tbsp slivered almonds

How to make it

  1. Blend cottage cheese, vanilla and sweetener until smooth.
  2. Divide into two bowls; top each with half the berries and almonds.

Workout version: half of one full-size snack + one Fairlife shake (30 g protein / 150 calories), about 315 calories. The grocery list covers full-size snacks.
